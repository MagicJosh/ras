Charts on a panel grid


Panels on 12 columns that widen on a tablet and stack on a phone. Each panel header names the chart, its unit, the example tag and the source. Inline: parts.css "panel grid" and "charts", and chart.js (plus data.js for the table twin). In the calls, S[0] to S[5] are var(--s1) to var(--s6), rnd is a seeded CrispChart.rng, and T0 and MIN are a start time and one minute in ms.
